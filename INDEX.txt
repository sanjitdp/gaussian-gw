Code supplement: Optimal Transportation and Alignment Between Gaussian Measures

We include implementations of Gaussian IGW alignment and multimarginal
Gaussian optimal transport, experiment scripts, notebooks, and numerical
result summaries. See README.md for installation and reproduction commands.

Contents
--------
README.md: setup, numerical methods, and experiment commands.
requirements.txt: Python dependencies.
LICENSE: terms for use and redistribution.
src/*.py: numerical solvers, experiment runners, plotting, and validation.
src/*.ipynb: examples and notebook experiment workflows.
src/math.mplstyle: plotting style.
src/cache/*.csv and *.json: recorded measurements and numerical checks.
src/cache/clustering/metadata.json: clustering model and experiment settings.
